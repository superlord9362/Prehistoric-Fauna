Each fossil type provides 
different fossils from the animals 
that lived during their respective 
time period. The skulls can be 
mounted by themselves, or 
combined with 8 Fossilized Bones to 
make a proper skeletal mount of 
the animal. However, there is no 
guarantee that you’ll find bones, 
and you may only find shells or 
even just rock!

Fossils can also be surrounded 
by sticks to create Wall Fossils! 
These can be hung up to display 
different fossil slabs.
