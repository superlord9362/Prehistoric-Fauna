The day of my expedition draws 
near! I've been gathering the 
resources needed to craft myself 
a new Geological Hammer and a 
Paleontology Table. These are 
both important tools for any 
budding paleontologist, and 
needed in any fossil hunting 
expedition!

Using a regular pickaxe has a high 
chance of destroying the fossil 
hidden within the stone, but with a 
geological hammer, one will always 
get a fossil.

Fossils can be found at different 
levels, with older fossils being 
found deeper underground. 
Cretaceous fossils can be found 
in chalk, while Jurassic and 
Triassic fossils can be found in 
siltstone and sandstone 
respectively. Once fossils are 
collected, one will need a 
paleontology table for cleaning 
them!
