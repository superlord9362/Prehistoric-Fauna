Found In: Chinle

Drops: 1-3 Large Raw 
Archosauromorph

Diet: Carnivore

Breeding Item: Large Raw Synapsid

Max Health: 20

Max Hunger: 50

Temperaments: Poposaurus has an 
80% chance of being territorial, a 
15% chance of being protective, 
and a 5% chance of being 
aggressive.














Sleep Cycle: Cathemeral

Behavior: Poposaurus is one of 
the two major predators of the 
Chinle Formation. Unlike 
Postosuchus, Poposaurus is a 
pursuit predator, actively chasing 
its prey down.
