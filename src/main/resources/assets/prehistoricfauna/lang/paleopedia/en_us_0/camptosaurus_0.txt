Found In: Morrison

Drops: 1-2 Large Raw Ornithopod

Diet: Herbivore

Breeding Item: Cladophlebis

Max Health: 40

Max Hunger: 100

Temperaments: Camptosaurus has 
an 85% chance of being protective, 
and a 15% chance of being 
territorial.

Sleep Cycle: Diurnal

Behavior: Camptosaurus can be 
tamed by feeding it Cladophlebis. 
Once tamed, it can be saddled, 
chested, and ridden. Other tamed 
Camptosaurus will follow any 
Camptosaurus that is being ridden.
